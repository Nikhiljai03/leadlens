## What is Caprae's mission?
Caprae Capital wants to do more than provide money to acquisition entrepreneurs. Unlike most private equity firms that rely on financial engineering, Caprae aims to transform the businesses it backs through strategic initiatives, with AI adoption as a key part. Its SaaS (Search-as-a-Service / software) and MaaS (M&A as a Service) models treat an acquisition as a seven-year journey, where most value is created after closing, not at the deal.

Caprae also builds its own tools, such as SaaSquatch, instead of renting someone else's, so that its support is data-driven and fully in its control. The goal is to turn good businesses into great ones.

## How is Caprae changing the ETA space and broader PE?
In Entrepreneurship Through Acquisition (ETA), searchers usually have to build their own sourcing machine: lists, cold outreach, CRM and benchmarks. Caprae packages that into a service, with a full outreach stack, a 30+ person cold-calling team, shared conversion benchmarks and its own sourcing software. This lowers the cost and time of finding a business to buy.

In broader PE, Caprae stays involved after the acquisition and builds software to support operators, where the industry norm is funding plus quarterly check-ins. Using AI to improve decisions and operations in smaller, lower-middle-market companies is a different model from financial engineering.
